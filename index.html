Design Philosophy

Why most UIs fail long-term

// 01

Structure Over Decoration

Clean semantic HTML. No inline styles. Ever. CSS architecture that scales. BEM methodology. Components that compose, not collide. The foundation matters more than the paint.

// 02

Tokens, Not Magic Numbers

Every spacing, color, shadow, and radius comes from a defined system. Design tokens aren't optional—they're the contract between design and code. Consistency is automated, not hoped for.

// 03

Vanilla JS Sometimes Wins

Frameworks solve problems. But not every problem needs a framework. Understanding the platform means knowing when 50 lines of vanilla JS beats 5000 lines of dependencies.

// 04

Accessibility is Architecture

ARIA labels, semantic markup, keyboard navigation, reduced motion preferences—these aren't afterthoughts. They're structural decisions that happen at the architecture level, not the QA level.
